Walk to wake up. Move to stop.

How the alarm works

Moove is an alarm clock with no mercy on snoozers: when the alarm fires, the only way to stop it is to get up and walk the number of steps you set (10–100). Your step count is shown live on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, and a vigorous shake counts as a step if walking isn't possible.

Alarm didn't fire?

  • Make sure the alarm is toggled on and the time is set for the correct AM/PM.
  • Moove alarms are scheduled through the system alarm engine, so they fire even if the app is closed, your phone is on silent, or Focus / Do Not Disturb is on. You still need to keep motion & fitness permission on (Settings → Moove → Motion & Fitness) so the wake-up walk can be counted.
  • If you restarted your phone right before the alarm, give it a moment — scheduled alarms re-arm on their own.

Steps not counting?

  • Hold or wear the phone naturally — your normal arm swing is enough; you don't need to shake hard.
  • Check that Motion & Fitness permission is enabled for Moove.
  • A firm shake of the phone also counts as a step.

Subscription & free trial

Every feature is free during your 7-day trial. After the trial, a Moove subscription (monthly or yearly) keeps your alarms working. You can manage or cancel anytime in Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions. If you restore the app or switch phones, use Restore purchases on the paywall to get your subscription back.

Import your own alarm sound

In alarm settings, choose Custom sound and pick an audio file from your device. The file stays on your device.
