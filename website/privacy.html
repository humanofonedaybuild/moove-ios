Moove Alarm Clock

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Moove Alarm Clock is built to wake you up and get you moving — and to handle your data with the same restraint. This policy explains what the app collects, what it never collects, and who processes what little there is.

What stays on your device

  • Motion & step data. Your steps and movement are measured by your iPhone's motion processor (CoreMotion) and processed entirely on the device. Step data is never sent to any server.
  • Alarms and preferences. Your alarm schedules, step goals, and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Imported audio. Any alarm sounds you import remain on your device.

What is processed for purchases

  • Subscription transactions. If you subscribe, your purchase is processed by Apple (App Store / StoreKit). We use RevenueCat, an industry-standard subscription management service, to validate purchases and keep your premium status in sync. In connection with this, RevenueCat processes transaction identifiers and subscription status (such as expiry and renewal events) on our behalf. This purchase data is used solely to run the subscription you paid for — it is not used for advertising and it is not sold.

What Moove never does

  • No advertising and no ad SDKs.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No collection of your name, email, contacts, photos, or location.
  • No health-data uploads — steps are used in the moment, on the device, to count your wake-up mission.

Third parties

  • Apple / App Store — handles payment and your subscription (Apple's own privacy policy applies to your Apple ID and payment data).
  • RevenueCat — validates subscription receipts so your premium features keep working. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com for details on their handling of transaction data.

Data deletion

Because nearly everything lives on your device, deleting the Moove app removes your data. To end any server-side subscription records, cancel the subscription in your App Store settings (or contact us below) and we will purge the RevenueCat record.
