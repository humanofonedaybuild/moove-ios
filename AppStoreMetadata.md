## Description

Moove is the alarm clock that guarantees you actually get out of bed. Instead of a snooze button, you walk a set number of steps to stop the alarm — starting your day with movement, not frustration.

**How it works:**
1. Set your alarm and choose a step goal (10–100 steps)
2. When the alarm fires, the only way to stop it is to get up and walk
3. Steps are counted by your iPhone's motion coprocessor (or by shaking your phone)
4. Track your progress on the Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, and Apple Watch
5. Mission complete — you're up, moving, and ready for the day

**Key Features:**
- AlarmKit-powered: alarms fire reliably through Do Not Disturb, Focus modes, Silent switch, and even if the app was force-closed
- Real-time step tracking: CoreMotion pedometer counts every step
- Shake detection: a vigorous shake also counts as a step — no cheating by moving the phone slowly
- Apple Watch companion: wrist-based step tracking with workout session background runtime
- Live Activities: Dynamic Island and Lock Screen show your step countdown in real time
- 5 built-in alarm sounds: choose from Classic, Gentle Wake, Urgent, Nature, or Digital
- Custom sounds: import your own audio files
- One snooze per alarm (configurable) — for emergencies only
- Clean, minimal interface — no clutter, no ads

## Privacy Policy

Moove collects minimal data to provide the alarm service:

- **Step count data** is processed on-device via CoreMotion and is not sent to any server
- **Subscription status** is managed through Apple's StoreKit 2 and is not stored on external servers
- **No personal information**, location data, or health data is collected or transmitted
- **No analytics or tracking SDKs** are used
- **Audio files you import** remain on your device
- **Alarm schedules and preferences** are stored locally in UserDefaults (App Group)

Moove does not share any user data with third parties.
